What to check before you order

Confirm the exact option and size

Save the product link, color and size together. For apparel, compare chest width, body length and sleeve length against a garment that fits you. For shoes, check whether the chart lists foot length or insole length before comparing centimeters.

Review warehouse QC photos

When photos are available, match the label, color and option to your order. Check visible seams, closures and obvious damage. If a measurement matters, ask for a photo with a ruler and confirm the agent’s exchange deadline.

Compare shipping quotes

Ask for packed dimensions, actual weight and the carrier’s billable weight. Compare quotes for the same destination and parcel. Record included services and any taxes or handling charges shown separately.

Estimate your total in USD

Use quotes for your destination. Leave unknown charges blank and add them once you have estimates.

JoyaGoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is a directory of product links used to compare finds before ordering through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses selected listings from CAFANS.

Where do the two main buttons go?

Both open the CAFANS homepage. The product links open individual CAFANS listings, where you can review details and see any available agent options.

Do the listed USD prices include shipping?

The cards show item reference prices. Check the current item price, agent charges and shipping quote for your selected option and destination.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check the listing and the agent’s order page for available photos. Ask which warehouse photos are provided for your order and when you can review them.
